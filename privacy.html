Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

The short version: MicroShift doesn't collect any personal information. Everything you add stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

This policy explains what the MicroShift app (“MicroShift”, “we”, “us”) does with information. It applies to the MicroShift app for iPhone and iPad.

Information we collect

None. MicroShift has no servers, no accounts and no internet connection of its own. We can't see your assignments, your steps, your scans or anything else you do in the app.

What the app stores on your device

To work, MicroShift saves these things in its own storage on your device:

  • The assignments you add: their titles, the instructions you typed, pasted or scanned, and the steps made from them.
  • Your progress: which steps you've finished and how long you actively spent on them.
  • Your pacing: how long kinds of work take you, so time estimates can match your pace.
  • The class names you create, and your settings.

This information never leaves your device through MicroShift. You can delete it at any time (see Your choices).

Camera and photos

If you scan an assignment, MicroShift asks to use the camera. If you choose photos instead, Apple's photo picker gives MicroShift only the photos you pick, not your whole library. Pages are read on your device. MicroShift keeps the text it finds, not the pictures.

Apple Intelligence

On devices that support it, MicroShift can use Apple Intelligence to understand your instructions. It uses Apple's on-device model, so your text is processed on your device and isn't sent to Apple or to us.

Dictation

If you dictate with your keyboard's microphone button, that's a feature of iOS, handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. MicroShift only receives the finished text, like anything else you type.

Backups

If you back up your device with iCloud Backup or to a computer, those backups can include MicroShift's data, like the data of your other apps. Backups are controlled by you and Apple, and we can't access them.

No tracking, ads or third parties

MicroShift contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code that collects data. We don't track you across apps or websites, and we never sell or share information, because we don't have any.

Children and students

MicroShift is made for students, including children under 13. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, there's nothing for parents to consent to under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). MicroShift doesn't receive education records from schools, and we never share anything with schools, teachers or anyone else. Parents can see everything MicroShift stores by opening the app on their child's device.

Your choices

  • Delete an assignment: swipe left on it on the home screen and tap Delete. It stays in Settings → Recently Deleted for 30 days and is then deleted for good. To delete it sooner, tap Delete Now there.
  • Reset your pacing: Settings → Advanced → Pacing → Reset Learned Pacing.
  • Delete everything: delete the MicroShift app. This removes all of its data from your device.
  • Camera: you can turn camera access on or off at any time in the iOS Settings app.

If you contact us

If you email us, we'll receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, never share it, and delete the conversation when it's no longer needed.

Changes to this policy

If MicroShift ever starts handling information differently, we'll update this policy and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.
