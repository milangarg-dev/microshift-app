Big work, one step at a time.

MicroShift turns any assignment into small, timed steps, so it's easier to start and easier to finish. Made for students with ADHD, dyslexia and dyscalculia, and anyone who finds it hard to know where to start.

From one big assignment to one small step.

No more staring at a long assignment, wondering where to begin.

Scan the pages, upload a file, type, paste or dictate the instructions, or share it from another app.

The assignment

“Write a 5 paragraph essay explaining the causes of World War I. Focus on militarism, alliances, imperialism, and nationalism.”

What MicroShift made

  1. Reread the prompt and underline what it asks 4 min
  2. Brainstorm ideas about the causes of World War I 6 min
  3. Outline your main points 6 min
  4. Write the introduction 8 min
  5. Write body paragraph 1 about militarism 10 min
  6. Write body paragraph 2 about alliances 10 min
  7. Write body paragraph 3 about imperialism and nationalism 10 min
  8. Write the conclusion 8 min
  9. Proofread and fix mistakes 10 min

About 72 minutes in all, in 9 small steps.2

Add anything

Bring in work the way it reaches you.

A printed worksheet, a PDF from your teacher, a screenshot, or a message from a group chat. MicroShift shows you the text it found before anything is made.

  • Scan pages Every page of a handout, straightened and read on your iPhone.
  • Choose photos Pick pictures of assignments you've already taken.
  • Upload files PDF, Word, Markdown and text files, up to 25 MB each.
  • Type, paste or dictate Use your keyboard's microphone to say it out loud.
  • Share to MicroShift In Classroom, Mail, Safari, Photos or Files, tap Share, then MicroShift. It's waiting in MicroShift already named, with the due date filled in when the assignment says one.

Smart breakdown

It reads messy instructions, so you don't have to.

Long teacher posts full of rules, texting shorthand, UK and other Englishes. MicroShift finds the actual work and turns it into steps. It works for chores, errands and applications too.

Small, timed steps

Big steps are split into pieces of about 10 minutes, each with its own time.

Every question counts

Each question on a worksheet, or topic on a revision checklist, gets its own step.

Learns your pace

Time estimates adjust to how long steps really take you.2 Prefer no clocks? Hide them.

Apple Intelligence, on your device

On supported iPhones and iPads, Apple Intelligence helps MicroShift understand messy instructions, right on the device.1 It never writes answers or does your work.

Works on every supported device

Without Apple Intelligence, MicroShift's own built-in engine makes every step, on iOS 17 and later.

Focus Mode

Just the step you're on.

One step fills the screen, in big, clear type. No countdowns, no alarms. If a step runs long, a quiet check-in asks if you're still working.

  • Read Step Aloud Hear the step instead of reading it.
  • Break Down Further Still too big? Split any step into smaller ones, and put it back together anytime.
  • Mark Complete & Continue Finish a step and the next one is ready. Go back anytime.

Stay on track

Gentle reminders. Real progress.

Due dates, reminders and a streak that keep you moving without the stress.

Due dates, soonest first

Give assignments a due date and time, and Home lists what's due soonest first. Late work is worded calmly: “Was due…”.

Reminders when you want them

A reminder 24 hours before work is due, or pick 12 hours, 2 days, 3 days or a week. Plus a study reminder on weekdays, at the time you choose.3

A streak that's kind

Finish one step on a weekday and the day counts. Weekends are off, so they never break your streak. Set a goal and watch your progress.

Achievements to celebrate

18 achievements, from your first task to 1,000 weekdays in a row. One of them is a secret. Each one arrives with a celebration.

Sorted by class

Group assignments by class, in the order you like. Hold a card to move it.

See your progress

Statistics shows your assignments and how many steps you've finished.

Undo a delete

Deleted something by mistake? It stays in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Accessibility

Designed for the way you think.

Made with ADHD, dyslexia and dyscalculia in mind: calm colors, big clear text and one thing at a time. And it works with the accessibility features built into your iPhone and iPad.

  • Low-sensory design Quiet colors, no flashing, no countdowns, no guilt.
  • Larger Text Every screen works at the largest text sizes.
  • Read Step Aloud Hear any step read out loud.
  • VoiceOver Every button and screen has a clear label.
  • Voice Control Say what you see to tap it.
  • Reduce Motion Animations and confetti calm down when it's on.
  • Dark Mode Easy on the eyes at night.
  • Clear contrast Text and buttons are easy to tell apart.
  • Guided tours A short tour of each screen the first time you open it.

Privacy

Your work stays on your device.

MicroShift doesn't collect any personal information. Scanning, reading and making steps all happen on your iPhone or iPad, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

  • No accounts Nothing to sign up for or sign in to.
  • No ads The app has no advertising.
  • No tracking No analytics, and nothing that follows you around.
  • Works offline No internet connection needed.
  • Stays on your device Your assignments, scans and progress never leave it.
  • Made for students Built for students of every age, and collects nothing from anyone.

Data Transfer

New device? Bring everything.

Move your assignments, progress, settings, pace, streak and achievements to another iPhone or iPad. No account, no cloud: it goes straight from one device to the other.

  • Scan QR codes Export shows a few codes; scan them on your new device, in any order.
  • Or a backup file For years of work, save one file and send it with AirDrop or Files.
  • Merge or replace Keep what's already there or start fresh. Achievements from both devices are kept.
