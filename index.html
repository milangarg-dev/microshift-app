MicroShift Support

Big work, one step at a time.

MicroShift turns an assignment into small, doable steps. Scan the pages or type the instructions, and it lays out one step at a time, with a calm timer and a way to break any step down further. It was made for students with ADHD, dyslexia and dyscalculia, and for anyone who finds it hard to know where to start.

Need help? Email milangargdev@gmail.com. We read every message and will get back to you as soon as we can.

Common questions

Where is my information stored?

Only on your device. MicroShift has no accounts and no servers, and nothing you add is sent to us or anyone else. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

How do I delete an assignment?

On the home screen, swipe left on the assignment and tap Delete. It moves to Settings → Recently Deleted for 30 days, in case you change your mind, and is then deleted for good. To delete it sooner, open Recently Deleted and tap Delete Now. To delete every assignment at once, go to Settings → General → Delete All Assignments. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. Everything, including scanning and making steps, happens on your device.

What does Apple Intelligence do in MicroShift?

On iPhones and iPads that support Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 or later), MicroShift can use Apple's on-device model to understand messy instructions better. It runs on your device too, and MicroShift uses it automatically when it's available. Without it, MicroShift uses its own built-in engine, which works on every supported device. To use the built-in engine instead, turn off Use Apple Intelligence in Settings → Advanced.

What can I upload?

Photos of your assignment, and PDF, Word (.docx), Markdown (.md) and text (.txt) files up to 25 MB each. Open Upload, then Choose Photos or Choose Files. MicroShift shows the text it found in each one before you tap Submit. A PDF that's only a picture of a page is read like a scan.

What languages does MicroShift support?

English only, for now. The app is in English, and it turns assignments written in English into steps, including UK and other Englishes and texting shorthand.

Tips for scanning

  • Lay the page flat in good light, and avoid glare from a lamp or window.
  • Fit the whole page in the photo. You can add several pages for one assignment.
  • Check the text MicroShift found before you tap Submit. If a page came out wrong, remove it and try again, or type the instructions instead.

Can I change the steps or the time estimates?

Steps and times are suggestions. Tap Break Down Further on any step to get smaller steps. MicroShift learns how long things take you as you use it, and you can reset what it has learned in Settings → Advanced → Pacing.

Is MicroShift a medical tool?

No. MicroShift is a study helper. It doesn't diagnose, treat or replace care for any condition.
